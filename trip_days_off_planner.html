Connectivity Plan

Approach: A European eSIM (data + hotspot) as the main connection, paired with free Wi-Fi Calling on the AT&T line so your real number stays reachable for customer and team calls — cheaper and more flexible than relying on AT&T's Day Pass alone.

AT&T International Day Pass

$12/day per line, only charged on days you use the phone. Capped at 10 days ($120) per billing cycle — but the cap resets if the trip spans two billing cycles, so it could hit $120 twice. Keeps your number active with no new setup.

Airalo Eurolink Unlimited eSIM

~$35 per 10 days · 3GB/day at full speed, then throttled (not cut off) — plenty for calls and normal use. Two stacked passes cover the whole trip for roughly $70 total, well under the AT&T cap either way.

Setup not started yet

  • Buy the Airalo Eurolink Unlimited eSIM — size it to cover Sep 6–23 (two 10-day passes, ~$70 total)
  • Turn on Wi-Fi Calling on the AT&T line before leaving the US (Settings → Cellular → Wi-Fi Calling)
  • Install the eSIM profile at home over Wi-Fi — don't activate/start the clock until landing in France
  • Test it at home: Airplane Mode + Wi-Fi only, confirm a call/text still goes through over Wi-Fi Calling
  • Turn AT&T cellular data/roaming OFF before departure so the Day Pass doesn't quietly trigger on top of the eSIM
  • Decide if your partner needs her own eSIM (recommended if you'll split up during the day) and buy it
  • Check your AT&T billing cycle date — only matters if Day Pass ends up as backup, since the trip may span two cycles
  • On landing in Paris: activate the eSIM data plan, confirm Wi-Fi Calling shows "connected," test one real call or text

Paris → Nice ~5.5–6 hrs

Train

Direct TGV from Gare de Lyon to Nice Ville. One train, no changes.

⚠Reserve seats ahead; keep bags with you, not overhead.

Nice → Portofino ~4–4.5 hrs

Train Ferry

No train reaches Portofino directly. Train from Nice to Genova (usually a change at Ventimiglia), then a regional train to Santa Margherita Ligure–Portofino station. From there, skip the local bus and take the short ferry (Golfo Paradiso line) into Portofino — more scenic and avoids the bus.

⚠Watch bags closely at Ventimiglia/Genova stations; only take the ferry in good weather.

Portofino → Pisa ~2.5–3 hrs

Train

Regional/intercity train from Santa Margherita Ligure down the coast to Pisa Centrale, typically one change around La Spezia.

⚠Low-risk regional train; just keep tickets validated if required.

Pisa → Rome ~3 hrs

Train

Direct high-speed train (Frecciabianca/Frecciargento). Straightforward, no changes.

⚠Easy leg, no real risk.

Rome → Amalfi ~3.5–4 hrs total

Train Ferry

High-speed Frecciarossa from Rome to Salerno (~2.5–2.75 hrs), then a ferry from Salerno to Amalfi (~35–70 min). The ferry runs roughly April–October and is faster and far more comfortable than the winding SITA coastal bus. Outside ferry season, a private car transfer is the better fallback over the public bus.

⚠Rome Termini is a known pickpocket spot — use a crossbody bag; confirm the ferry is running before counting on it.

Amalfi → Milan / Lake Como ~5–5.5 hrs total

Ferry Train

Ferry or transfer back to Salerno or Naples, then a direct Frecciarossa high-speed train to Milano Centrale (~4–4.5 hrs from Naples). From Milan, a short regional train to Como (~35–40 min).

⚠Long travel day — keep passports and valuables on you, not in checked bags.

General Safety — Whole Trip

  • Split cash/cards between you two — don't carry everything in one bag
  • Use a crossbody or zip-close bag in crowded spots (train stations, Rome, Nice's old town)
  • Stick to official taxi ranks or rideshare apps — skip unlicensed drivers
  • Keep digital + physical copies of passports and reservations
  • Keep your phone/data connection on so you can reach each other if you split up
